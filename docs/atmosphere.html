Physical rendering

Atmosphere, stars, clouds & oceans

The sky emerges from shared physical parameters and stellar illumination rather than a skybox.

Atmospheric scattering

Rayleigh, Mie/aerosols and absorption feed transmittance, multi-scattering, sky-view and aerial-perspective LUTs.

parameters → transmittance → multi-scatter → sky-view → aerial perspective

Clouds

Spherical shells combine low-frequency weather coverage with 3D detail fields; temporal reprojection amortizes raymarch cost.

Ocean

Long range uses a spherical reflective surface. Near field can add compute-driven spectral waves.

Stars

Radius, temperature and luminosity drive black-body-inspired color, limb darkening and procedural granulation. Corona and flares are visual approximations.
