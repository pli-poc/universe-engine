Architecture & coordinate model

Astrava separates authoritative simulation coordinates from render coordinates.

Reference-frame hierarchy

UniverseFrame (Float64) └─ StarSystemFrame ├─ StarFrame └─ PlanetOrbitalFrame └─ PlanetRotatingFrame └─ SurfaceFrame └─ LocalPhysicsFrame └─ Camera-relative RenderFrame (Float32)

A surface building remains defined in its rotating planet frame. The planet transform changes; the building does not need an astronomical position rewrite every frame.

Precision contract

DomainRepresentationPurpose
